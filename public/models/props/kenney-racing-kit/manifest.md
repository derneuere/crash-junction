- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit (direct zip: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/racing-kit/933b8fd9fd-1677580949/kenney_racing-kit.zip)
- License: CC0 1.0 (see License.txt) — no attribution required
- Format: self-contained .glb (textures embedded; no external files needed)

Native scale is Kenney "track tile" units (1.0 = one road tile). For GANTRY POINT
(player car ~4.5 m sedan) multiply by roughly 6-10x; per-file suggestions below.
All models sit on the ground at y=0 (origin at base).
